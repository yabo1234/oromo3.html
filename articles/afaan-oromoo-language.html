Afaan Oromoo — also called Oromiffa or Oromo — is spoken by more than 40 million people, making it the most widely spoken language in the Cushitic branch of the Afroasiatic family and one of the most spoken languages on the African continent. It is the mother tongue of the Oromo people, the largest ethnic group in Ethiopia, and is today written using the Latin-based Qubee alphabet introduced in 1991.

The Qubee Alphabet: A Turning Point

Before Qubee, Afaan Oromoo had no standardised writing system widely accepted by its speakers. The adoption of the Latin-based Qubee script in 1991 was a landmark moment: it made the language accessible to write and learn, enabled the publication of books, newspapers, and government documents in Afaan Oromoo, and became a powerful symbol of Oromo cultural and political identity.

Today, schoolchildren across Oromia learn to read and write in Afaan Oromoo from their earliest years, and the language is used in regional administration, courts, and broadcast media.

Structure and Distinctiveness

Afaan Oromoo is a tonal language with a rich system of vowel length and consonant gemination (doubling). Its grammar is verb-final (subject–object–verb), and it makes extensive use of suffixes to express grammatical relationships. The language has numerous dialects — including Borana, Guji, Macha, Tulama, and Harar — while a standard form has been developed for education and media.

Linguistic Justice and the Road Ahead

Despite its size and vitality, Afaan Oromoo faces ongoing challenges. Advocates argue that the language deserves recognition as a federal working language in Ethiopia alongside Amharic, greater representation in higher education, and increased support for digital tools — dictionaries, keyboards, and natural language processing systems — that would enable it to thrive in the modern information environment.

Afaan Oromoo — Oromiffa ykn Oromo jedhamuus beekama — namoota miliyoona 40 ol irraan dubbifama, kan kun afaan hunda caalaa Afaan Kushitikii fi Afaan Afroaasiiyaa keessaa dubbatamu gochaa. Saba Oromoo, kan uummata guddaa Itoophiyaa ta'e, asal-dhalootaa dha, fi har'a qubee Latin irratti hundaa'e — Qubee bara 1991 keessummeeffame — keessatti barreeffama.

Qubee: Yeroo Jijjiirrama

Dursee Qubee, Afaan Oromoo sirna barreeffama tokkummaa dubbattoota isaa daran fudhatan hin qabu ture. Qubee qubee Laatiinii irratti hundaa'e bara 1991 fudhachuun yeroo seenaa qabeessa ture: afaan dubbisuu fi barachuu salphise, maxxansalee, gaazexaalee fi galmee mootummaa Afaan Oromootiin dhiheessuun danda'ame, fi mallattoo eenyummaa aadaa fi siyaasaa Oromoo ta'e.

Har'a, barattooti mana barumsaa Oromiyaa guutuu keessatti bara jalqabaatii Afaan Oromootiin dubbisuu fi barreessuu barachuu, fi afaan naannoo bulchiinsaa, mana murtii fi midiyaa damsaa keessatti fayyadamama.

Caasaa fi Addummaa

Afaan Oromoo afaan toonii fi sirna dheerdaa fi gabaabbina sagalee fi lakkoofsa dabalataa (duplicating) qabu dha. Guraamaatikaan isaa sagalee-dhumaa (mata — waa'ee — sagalee) dha, fi xumuraalee hedduu itti fayyadama. Afaanichaatti gosa hedduun jira — Booranaa, Gujii, Macaa, Tuulamaa fi Harar — yeroo tokkos gosa idilee barnoota fi midiyaatiif guddifame.

Mirgaa Afaanii fi Karaa Fuula Dura

Bal'ina fi jireenya isaa irrattis, Afaan Oromoo rakkoolee itti fufa. Bakka bu'ooti Afaan Oromoo afaan hojii federaalaa Itoophiyaa keessa Amaariffaa waliin ta'uuf beekamuu, barnoota olaanaa keessatti gosa guddaa ta'uu fi meeshaalee dijitaalaa deeggarsaaf — galmee jechootaa, qubee mana itti barreeffamu fi sirna qubee ofumaa — deeggarsaa guddaa akka argatu gaafatu.
