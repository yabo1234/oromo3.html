Oromo Diaspora Raises Funds for Drought Relief

Diasporaa

Community organisations across North America and Europe have launched coordinated fundraising campaigns to support families in Oromia affected by prolonged drought conditions. The effort brings together diaspora associations, faith communities, and online giving platforms to channel emergency relief directly to affected households.

A United Response Across Continents

Oromo diaspora groups in cities including Minneapolis, Toronto, London, and Frankfurt have held fundraising events, online drives, and community dinners to pool resources. Funds collected are being directed to vetted local partner organisations working on the ground in drought-affected woredas in Oromia.

"When our people suffer, we act — wherever we are in the world," said one organiser based in the United States. "This is what community means."

The Scale of the Crisis

Parts of Oromia have experienced below-average rainfall for consecutive seasons, leading to failed harvests, livestock losses, and food insecurity for tens of thousands of families. Aid organisations have called for urgent assistance to prevent the situation from worsening before the next planting season.

The diaspora fundraising effort complements — rather than replaces — appeals to international humanitarian organisations and government bodies. Organisers emphasise the importance of long-term development investment alongside immediate relief.

How to Help

Those wishing to contribute are encouraged to reach out to established diaspora organisations in their region and to verify that donations are directed through transparent, accountable channels. This article will be updated with specific giving links as they are confirmed.

Dhaabbattooti hawaasaa Ameerikaa Kaabaa fi Awurooppaa guutuu sagantaalee maallaqaa waliin qindaa'an jalqabanii maatii Oromiyaa keessatti hoo'a biyya dhibuun miidhaman gargaaruuf sosso'aa jiru. Sagantaan kun waldaalee diasporaa, hawaasota amantii fi tekinoolojiilee kennaa interneetii walitti fidee gargaarsa hatattamaa maatiilee miidhamaniif geessisa.

Deebii Tokkummaa Addaa Addaa Irraa

Gareeleen Oromoo diasporaa magaaloota akka Minneapolis, Toronto, London fi Frankfurt keessatti sagantaalee maallaqaa, sochiilee interneetii fi nyaata hawaasaa qopheessanii qabeenya walitti qabataniiru. Maallaqi walitti qabame dhaabbattoota naannoo galmaa'aniif hojjetaniif woreedaalee Oromiyaa hoo'a biyya dhibuun miidhaman keessatti ergamaa jira.

"Uummanni keenya gidiraa keessa jiraatee, kan meeqa addunyaa irra jirrulle hojjenna," jedha qindeessaan tokko Ameerikaa jiraatu. "Hawaasni kun jechuun kana."

Bal'ina Rakkoo Kanaa

Naannooleen Oromiyaa tokko tokko roobni gadi aanaa ta'u dheerina isaa daran itti fufe, kunis galii qonnaaleef miidha, beeyladaa dhabuu fi yaaddoo nyaataa maatii kuma hedduuf fide. Dhaabbattooti gargaarsaa haala kana duras taasisuuf gargaarsa hatattamaa kan waamuuf yeroo dhaabbata oomisha lammaffaa ta'utti.

Akkamitti Gargaaruu Dandeenya

Kenne barbaadan waldaalee diasporaa naannoo isaanitti beekkaman waliin quunnamuuf jajjabeeffamu. Barrichi walitti qabaa maallaaqaaleef liinkoota adda addaatiin haaromsama.
