Afaan Oromoo Literacy Program Reaches New Milestone

Barnoota

Sagantaa Barreeffama Afaan Oromoo Mallattoo Haaraa Bira Gahe

📅 April 22, 2026Ebla 22, 2026 ✍️ Oromo News & Views EditorialGulantaa Oduu fi Yaada Oromoo 🎓 5 min readDaqiiqaa 5 dubbisuu
🎓

A volunteer-led literacy initiative has crossed a landmark threshold: more than 50,000 adults across Oromia have now achieved functional literacy in Afaan Oromoo through the programme's community-based learning circles. Launched in 2021 by a coalition of teachers, diaspora donors, and local NGOs, the effort has grown into one of the most successful grassroots education campaigns in the Horn of Africa.

The Qubee Script and Its Significance

Afaan Oromoo was written in the Ge'ez (Ethiopic) script and then briefly in Arabic before the introduction of the Latin-based Qubee alphabet in 1991 — a development that transformed the cultural and political landscape. Qubee is phonetically intuitive, enabling learners to achieve basic reading and writing proficiency within weeks.

"Qubee was a revolution," said Kumsa Boru, one of the programme's lead trainers. "When our grandmothers pick up a booklet and read it for the first time in their lives — that is transformative. That is freedom."

How the Circles Work

The programme uses a peer-learning model: trained community facilitators — often secondary school graduates or university students — run weekly two-hour sessions in homes, community halls, and under trees. Each circle typically comprises 10–20 adults. After twelve weeks, participants sit an assessment and, upon passing, receive a certificate of Afaan Oromoo literacy.

Printed materials — primers, story booklets, and numeracy workbooks — are produced locally and distributed free of charge. The diaspora fundraising arm has now crowdfunded over USD 180,000 towards printing and facilitator stipends.

Beyond Reading: Economic Impact

Literacy gains are translating into tangible economic benefits. Participants who can now read market notices, contracts, and government forms report greater confidence in financial transactions and accessing government services. A small internal study found that graduates of the programme earned an average 12% more income than non-participants in the twelve months following graduation, largely due to improved access to markets and credit.

Women represent 62% of programme participants, reflecting both the historical gender gap in literacy and the community's recognition that investing in women's education yields multiplied returns for families and children.

Afaan Oromoo as Africa's Fourth Most Spoken Language

With an estimated 40–45 million native speakers, Afaan Oromoo is the fourth most spoken language on the African continent and the most widely spoken in the Cushitic language family. Despite this, it was systematically suppressed for much of the 20th century under successive Ethiopian administrations.

Today, Afaan Oromoo is a working language of the Oromia Regional State, is used in primary education throughout the region, and is increasingly present in media, music, and public life. Advocates argue, however, that full linguistic justice requires continued investment in adult literacy, higher education in the language, and its recognition at the federal level.

What Comes Next

Programme coordinators have set an ambitious goal: 200,000 adult learners by 2030. To reach that scale, they are pursuing partnerships with regional government education bureaux and international donors. A digital component is also being piloted — WhatsApp-based learning groups for areas with limited facilitator access.

"Every person who learns to read in Afaan Oromoo is a guardian of our language," said programme director Ibsaa Caalaa. "And a guardian of our future."

Sagantaa barnoota dammaqsummaa hoggansa fedhiin guuzameen mallattoo seenaa qabeessa qaqqabe: namoota gurguddaa kuma 50 ol ta'an Oromiyaa guutuu keessatti gara Afaan Oromoo barreeffama dalagaa argatan. Sagantaan bara 2021 karoorfame — mooraa barsiisotaa, maallaqni diasporaa fi dhaabbattoota hawaasaatiin hundeeffame — har'aa sagantaa barnootaa dirree inni daran milkaa'e tokkodha.

Qubee fi Baliinsa Isaa

Afaan Oromoo jalqaba qubee Ge'ez (Itoophikii) itti barreeffamaa ture, dhumarattis Arabiffaatiin, hanga qubeen Laatiinii Qubee jedhamuun beekamu bara 1991 keessummeessamutti. Qubeen fonetikaan salphaa waan ta'eef, barataan torbee muraasa keessatti dubbisuu fi barreessuu jalqabuu danda'a.

"Qubeen jijjiirrama guddaadha," jedha Kumsaa Boruu, leenjisaa sagantichaa. "Ni taatee olliitti barruu qabatee yeroo jalqabaatiif dubbisuu yeroo argutti — bilisummaa dha."

Gareen Barachuu Akkamiin Hojjeta?

Sagantaan kun gosa barachuu hiriyaadhaan guuzamu fayyadama: qindeessitoonni hawaasaa leenjifaman — barattoota sadarkaa lammaffaa ykn yuunivarsiitii — torban torbaniin sa'aatii lama gabaalee, manneen marii fi gannatti deemu. Gareewwan tokko tokko namoota gurguddaa 10–20 qabatu. Torbee kudha lama booda, hirmaattonni qormaata fudhatu fi darban waraqaa ragaa argatu.

Dhaabbilee Diinagdee Irrattis Dhiibbaa

Barnoota argachuun bu'aa diinagdee mul'isuu eegaleera. Hirmaattonni gabaa, kontiraataa fi galmee mootummaa dubbisuu kan danda'an guddina amanamummaa walitti qabinsaa fi tajaajila mootummaa argachuu irratti gabaassu. Shaakalli xiqqoo keessaa argame sagantaa keessaa darban galii dhibbentaa 12 ol dabalatatti arguu isaanii mul'iseera.

Dubartoonni hirmaattoota sagantaa 62% bakka bu'u — garaagarummaa barnoota heeyyama fi hawaasni maatii fi daa'imman keessa maallaqni dubartootarratti dhangalaasuun bu'aa guddaa akka fiddu beekuu isaa mul'isa.

Afaan Oromoo — Afaan Afrikaa Kan Ammayyaa

Dogoggora dubbattootaa miliyoona 40–45 ol kan qabu Afaan Oromoo afaan Afrikaa kan daran dubbatamuun daran gara afraneen jira fi afaan aadaa Kushitikii hunda caalaa dubbatamuudha. Bara dheeraaf mootummaalee Itoophiyaa garaa garaan dhiibamaa ture.

Har'a Afaan Oromoo afaan hojii Naannoo Oromiyaa ta'ee, barnoota sadarkaa jalqabaa naannoo guutuu keessatti tajaajila kennaa jira, akkasumas miidiyaa, muuziqaa fi jireenya hawaasaa keessatti guddataa jira.

Itti Aanee Maal Ta'a?

Qindeessitooti sagantaa karoora gurraacha qabataniiru: barattooti gurguddaa 200,000 hanga bara 2030. Kaayyoo kana bira gahuuf, walitti qunnamtii biiroo barnoota naannoo fi giddugaleessota addunyaa waliin hordofaa jiru. Sagantaa dijitaalaa tokkollee — gareewwan barachuu WhatsApp-based naannoo beeksisaa hin qabneen — yaalameera.

"Namni tokko tokko Afaan Oromootiin barachuu danda'u eegduu afaanichaa dha," jedha Ibsaa Caalaa, dareektariin sagantaa. "Eegduu futurisaa dha."
