Education

Afaan Oromoo literacy efforts reach more rural communities

Barnoota

Sochiin barnoota Afaan Oromoo hawaasa baadiyyaa dabalataa bira gahe

📅 May 7, 2026Caamsaa 7, 2026 ✍️ Oromo News & Views EditorialGulantaa Oduu fi Yaada Oromoo 🎓 5 min readDaqiiqaa 5 dubbisuu
🎓

Community-based Afaan Oromoo reading programs are expanding into more rural districts as volunteer teachers and local organisers report rising enrolment and stronger public interest. Education supporters say the growth reflects both practical local demand and a wider commitment to language, literacy, and cultural continuity.

Growth in Rural Areas

Organisers say new learning groups have opened in villages where adults previously had little access to mother-tongue reading support. Sessions are often held in schools after hours, community halls, or shared local spaces, making participation easier for working adults and parents.

Role of Volunteer Teachers

Much of the program's momentum comes from volunteer teachers, recent graduates, and local education advocates who help run reading circles and basic writing lessons. Their work has been especially important in areas where formal adult education services remain limited.

Community Impact

Local organisers say learners are using their new skills to read notices, complete forms, support children's schooling, and participate more confidently in public life. The spread of literacy in Afaan Oromoo is also seen as strengthening local identity and preserving the use of the language in everyday settings.

Education advocates say sustained support, printed materials, and local coordination will be key if the program is to continue reaching additional rural communities in the months ahead.

Sagantaaleen dubbisuu Afaan Oromoo hawaasa irratti hundaa'an aanaalee baadiyyaa dabalataa keessatti babal'achaa jiru; barsiisonni fedhiidhaan tajaajilan fi qindeessitoonni naannoo galmeen dabalaa fi fedhiin uummataa cimsaa jiraachuu ibsu. Deeggartoonni barnootaa akka jedhanitti, guddinni kun fedhii dhugaa hawaasaa fi kutannoo afaan, barnootaa fi aadaa eegsisuu calaqqisiisa.

Baadiyyaa Keessatti Babal'ina

Qindeessitoonni akka ibsanitti, gareewwan barnootaa haaraan gandoota keessatti banamaa jiru; achitti namoonni gurguddoon deeggarsa dubbisuu afaan haadha isaanii irratti hundaa'e kana dura argachuuf carraa xiqqaa qabu turan. Sagantaaleen kunneen yeroo baay'ee manneen barnootaa keessatti yeroo hojii booda, galma hawaasaa yookaan bakka waliinii keessatti geggeeffamu; kunis hirmaannaa salphisa.

Gahee Barsiisota Fedhii

Cimina sagantichaa keessaa qooda guddaan barsiisota fedhiidhaan tajaajilan, eebbifamtoota haaraa fi deeggartoota barnootaa naannoo irraa dhufa. Isaanis gareewwan dubbisuu fi barnoota barreessuu bu'uuraa gaggeessu; keessumaa bakka tajaajilli barnoota namoota gurguddoo hanqatu keessatti hojii isaanii murteessaa dha.

Bu'aa Hawaasaa

Qindeessitoonni naannoo akka jedhanitti, barattoonni ogummaa isaanii haaraa fayyadamuun beeksisa dubbisuu, guca guutuu, barnoota ijoollee deeggaruu fi jireenya hawaasaa keessatti ofitti amanamummaan hirmaachuu jalqaban. Babal'inni dubbisuu fi barreessuu Afaan Oromoo ammoo eenyummaa naannoo cimsuu fi afaanichaa jireenya guyyaa guyyaa keessatti tikfachuuf gargaaraa jira.

Deeggartoonni barnootaa deeggarsi itti fufiinsa qabu, maxxansa barnootaa fi qindoominni naannoo yoo jiraate qofa sagantaan kun hawaasa baadiyyaa dabalataa bira ga'uu danda'a jedhu.
