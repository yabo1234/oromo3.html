Somaliland Military Base – Key Points Summary

📅 April 6, 2026 📰 Source summary 🌍 Red Sea security
🛰️

This publication-ready summary outlines reported military construction in Berbera, Somaliland, and the strategic security context around the Gulf of Aden and the Bab al-Mandab strait.

Source: Le Monde, 6 April 2026
https://www.lemonde.fr/afrique/article/2026/04/06/au-somaliland-la-discrete-implantation-d-une-base-militaire-construite-par-les-emirats-pour-les-etats-unis-et-israel_6677457_3212.html

1. A discreet but significant military construction

Satellite images show that Berbera airport has been expanding since October 2025. The work is taking place out of public view, on the outskirts of Berbera.

2. A new base for three foreign powers

The airport is being transformed to host a military base for the UAE, the United States, and Israel — described as three allies of Somaliland.

3. Strategic location

Berbera sits at the southern entrance of the Red Sea, making it a highly strategic point for maritime control and regional military projection.

4. Link to Israel’s recognition of Somaliland

The construction coincides with Israel’s recognition of Somaliland’s independence on 26 December 2025, a diplomatic surprise and the first such recognition globally.

5. Israel’s strategic objective

Israel aims to establish a forward operating post in the Gulf of Aden, close to Yemen’s coast where Houthi rebels operate.

6. Regional conflict context

The Houthi movement, aligned with Iran, entered into open conflict with Israel on 28 March, threatening to block the Bab al-Mandab strait — a critical maritime chokepoint. This is described as a red line for both Israel and the United States.
