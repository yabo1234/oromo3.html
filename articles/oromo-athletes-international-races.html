Oromo athletes post strong results in international races

Ispoortii

Atileetonni Oromoo dorgommii idil-addunyaa keessatti bu'aa cimaa galmeessan

📅 May 4, 2026Caamsaa 4, 2026 ✍️ Oduu AI Afaan Oromoo EditorialGulantaa Oduu fi Yaada Oromoo

Distance runners from Oromia recorded standout performances in several recent international road and track events, drawing praise from supporters at home and across the diaspora.

Coaches and sports observers said the results reflect both individual discipline and the continued strength of local running traditions that have produced generations of elite athletes.

Community members noted that successful performances abroad often inspire younger runners, especially in towns where athletics remains one of the most visible pathways to opportunity and recognition.

Sports advocates are urging stronger investment in training facilities, youth competitions, and grassroots coaching so that the next generation of Oromo athletes can build on this momentum.

Fiigdoonni fageenya dheeraa Oromiyaa irraa dorgommii daandii fi pistaa idil-addunyaa muraasa keessatti bu'aa adda ta'e galmeessuun deeggartoota biyya keessaa fi diasporaa biratti dinqisiifannaa argataniiru.

Leenjistoonni fi hordoftoonni ispoortii akka jedhanitti, bu'aan kun cimina dhuunfaa qofaa osoo hin taane aadaa fiigicha naannoo beekamaa ta'ee fi dhaloota atileetota beekamoo hedduu horate agarsiisa.

Hawaasni akka ibsutti, bu'aan gaariin biyya alaa dargaggoota fiigicha irratti hirmaatan kakaasa; keessumaa magaalaalee keessatti atileetiksiin carraa fi beekamtii guddaa keessaa tokko ta'etti.

Deeggartoonni ispoortii dhaabbilee mootummaa fi hawaasa irraa iddoowwan leenjii, dorgommii dargaggootaa fi leenjii bu'uuraa irratti invastimantii jabaa akka taasifamu waamicha dhiyeessaniiru.
