Diaspora groups support drought-affected families

Diasporaa

Gareewwan diasporaa maatii hongeen miidhaman deeggaruu itti fufan

📅 May 6, 2026Caamsaa 6, 2026 ✍️ Oromo News & Views EditorialGulantaa Oduu fi Yaada Oromoo

Oromo community organisations in North America and Europe have expanded emergency fundraising campaigns to support families facing severe drought conditions across affected parts of Oromia.

Volunteers say the assistance includes food staples, clean water deliveries, school materials for children, and limited medical support coordinated with trusted local networks.

Organisers noted that community-led fundraising has become especially important where families are struggling with failed harvests, livestock losses, and rising prices for essential goods.

Several diaspora groups also called for more long-term partnerships focused on water access, drought resilience, and support for rural livelihoods so that emergency aid can be paired with sustainable recovery.

Waldaaleen hawaasa Oromoo Ameerikaa Kaabaa fi Awurooppaa keessa jiran maatii hongee cimaaf saaxilaman deeggaruuf hojii maallaqa walitti qabuu balaa ariifachiisaa babal'isaniiru.

Fedhii tajaajiltoonni jedhanitti deeggarsi kun nyaata bu'uuraa, bishaan qulqulluu geessisuu, meeshaalee barnootaa ijoolleef, fi deeggarsa yaalaa muraasa marsaa amanamaa naannoo waliin qindaa'e of keessatti qabata.

Qindeessitoonni akka ibsanitti, yeroo maatiin midhaan dhabuu, beellada dhabuufi gatiin meeshaalee bu'uuraa dabalaa jiru keessatti deeggarsi hawaasaan durfamu baay'ee murteessaa ta'ee jira.

Gareewwan diasporaa hedduun hojii yeroo dheeraa bishaan argachuu, hongee dandamachuu, fi jireenya baadiyyaa jabeessuu irratti xiyyeeffatu akka deeggarsa ariifachiisaa waliin walqabatu waamicha dhiyeessaniiru.
