Agricultural Reforms Set to Boost Smallholder Farmers

Diinagdee

Fooyyessa Qonnaa Qonnaawwan Xiqaa Gargaaruuf Qophaa'e

📅 May 2, 2026Caamsaa 2, 2026 ✍️ Oduu AI Afaan Oromoo EditorialGulantaa Oduu fi Yaada Oromoo 🌾 5 min readDaqiiqaa 5 dubbisuu
🌾

A sweeping set of agricultural policy reforms is poised to reshape the livelihoods of millions of smallholder farmers across Oromia. The initiative, backed by regional authorities and international development partners, targets improved seed access, cooperative market structures, and subsidised irrigation infrastructure.

Cooperative Models at the Centre

The cornerstone of the new approach is a network of farmer cooperatives that will pool resources and negotiate collectively with buyers. Pilot programs run in Jimma and West Hararghe zones in 2025 showed average yield increases of 28% for maize and 19% for wheat compared to the previous season, according to the regional Agriculture Bureau.

"When farmers work together they have real bargaining power," said Abebe Girma, a cooperative extension officer in Jimma. "Previously, middlemen captured most of the value. Now we are seeing farm-gate prices rise 15–20%."

Irrigation and Climate Resilience

Investment in small-scale irrigation schemes is central to the plan, given that rain-fed agriculture in parts of Oromia has become increasingly unreliable. A budget allocation of 1.2 billion birr over three years is earmarked for drip irrigation kits, communal water storage, and soil conservation earthworks.

Climate scientists have warned that without adaptation measures, crop failure rates in vulnerable lowland zones could increase by up to 40% by 2040. The reforms therefore include training programs on drought-tolerant seed varieties developed by Jimma University's College of Agriculture.

Challenges Ahead

Critics note that previous reform cycles faltered on implementation. "The policies are good on paper," said one civil society analyst who requested anonymity. "The challenge is always the last mile — getting the inputs to the farmer, ensuring extension workers are actually in the field, and preventing elite capture of cooperative leadership."

The government has pledged independent monitoring via a digital dashboard tracking key performance indicators at the woreda level, a step advocates hope will improve accountability.

What Farmers Are Saying

In Shashamane district, farmer Chaltu Dida expressed cautious optimism: "Last year we got the improved seeds on time. The fertilizer came late, but even so the harvest was better. If they fix the timing this year, we will be very happy."

For millions of households in Oromia whose livelihoods depend on agriculture, the stakes could not be higher. Early indications from the 2026 planting season suggest the reforms are beginning to take hold — but sustained implementation will be the true test.

Tarkaanfii fooyya'insaa qajeelfama qonnaa bal'aan Oromiyaa keessatti qonnaawwan xiqaa miliyoonotaa jiruu fi jireenyaa haguugguuf qophaa'eera. Karoorfamni kun, abbootii aangoo naannoo fi hirmaattoota misoomaa addunyaatiin deggarame, fooyyessa fayyadama sanyii, caasaalee gabaa kooperaatifii fi misooma bishaan qonnaa deeggaramee irratti xiyyeeffata.

Kooperaatifoonni Gidduu Jiru

Wantooti bu'uraa karooraa haaraa kanaa — Qabaannoo waltajjii kooperaatifii qonnaawwaanii kan humna walitti qabuufi fi bittaa fi gurgurtaa waloon raawwatu. Sagantaalee yaaloo kan Jimmaa fi naannoo Harargee Lixaa keessatti bara 2025 deemsiifame oomisha qamadii 19% fi boqqolloo 28% ol jijjiira argisisee, akka Biiroo Qonnaa Naannootti.

"Qonnaawwan yoo walitti qabamanii hojjetan humna murtii dhugaa qabu," jechuun Abeebee Girmaa, ogeessa kooperaatifii Jimmaa keessa hojjetu, ibse. "Duraan maamiltooti gidduu itti fayyadamanii gatiin baay'een isaa fudhatamuun ture. Amma gatiin qara goommaa 15–20% dabala."

Bishaan fi Cimina Qilleensaa

Maallaqni baajataa birrii biiliyoona 1.2 bara sadih keessatti meeshaalee bishaansumsaa xiqaa, kuusaa bishaan hawaasummaa fi eegumsa lafa irratti ramaduuf ramadameera. Qonnaawwan qilleensaatiin dhiibamaa jiran gargaaruuf tarkaanfiwwan haalduree dabalataa ni barbaachisu.

Saayintistoonni qilleensaa akka hubachiisanitti, yoo tarkaanfiin haaromsaa hin fudhatamne, rakkoo midhaan Oromiyaa naannoo bu'aa fi gadi keessatti haga 2040 dhibbentaa 40 ol dabalu. Fooyya'insonni kunneen kanaafi sanyii harcoo qilleensaa danda'u Yuunivarsiitii Jimmaa tiin Koollejjii Qonnaa irraa babbar'ame irratti leenjiiwwan dabalataa of keessaa qabu.

Rakkoolee Duraa

Qeeqattoonni fooyya'insoonni yeroo darbee raawwii irratti fashalaatti jedhu. "Qajeelfamoonni barreeffama keessatti gaariidha," jedha oggeessi hawaasa sivilii maqaa ishee of irraa qabuuf gaafatte. "Hanqinni yeroo hunda — sochii dhuma dhumaa — meeshaalee gara qonnaawwaniitti geessuuf, hojjettoonni bal'ina dirree keessa argamuu mirkaaneessuu fi qabsaa'aa kooperaatifii mo'atamuu dhorkuuf."

Qonnaawwan Maal Jedhu

Aanaa Shaashamannee keessa Caaltu Diidaa eegannoo eebbifameen ni dubbatti: "Waggaa darbeetti sanyii fooyya'aa yeroo isaa arganne. Mancaa'aan geeggeessuu isaanitti xiyyeeffachuu isaaniif, sagantaan fooyya'aa ta'e kan amma dhufaa jiruu fi gochaa jiru nu gammachiisa."
