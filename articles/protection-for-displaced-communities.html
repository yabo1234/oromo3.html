Civil groups urge protection for displaced communities

Mirga Namoomaa

Gareewwan sivikii hawaasota buqqaatan eegumsa akka argatan gaafatan

📅 May 5, 2026Caamsaa 5, 2026 ✍️ Oromo News & Views EditorialGulantaa Oduu fi Yaada Oromoo

Civil society organisations and community advocates are calling for stronger protection measures for families displaced by conflict, insecurity, and environmental pressure in affected parts of Oromia.

Advocates say urgent priorities include transparent compensation processes, safe and voluntary resettlement, and access to basic services such as shelter, food, education, and healthcare.

They also emphasised the need for independent monitoring so that reports of abuse, property loss, and barriers to return can be documented and addressed fairly.

Community leaders argue that long-term recovery will depend not only on emergency support, but also on policies that restore dignity, protect rights, and rebuild trust in local institutions.

Dhaabbileen sivikii fi falmitoonni hawaasaa maatii walitti bu'iinsa, nageenyi dhabamuu fi dhiibbaa naannoo irraa buqqaataniif tarkaanfii eegumsa jabaa akka fudhatamu gaafachaa jiru.

Akka falmitoonni jedhanitti, dhimmoonni ariifachiisoon kaffaltii iftoomina qabu, deebii qubsumaa nageenyaan fi fedhii isaanii irratti hundaa'e, akkasumas tajaajiloota bu'uuraa kan akka mana jireenyaa, nyaataa, barnootaa fi yaalaa ni dabalatu.

Isaanis gabaasni miidhaa, qabeenya dhabuu fi gufuuwwan deebi'anii qubachuu irratti mul'atan sirnaan galmaa'anii furmaata akka argatan hordoffii walabaa barbaachisaa ta'uu cimsanii ibsan.

Hoggantoonni hawaasaa akka amananitti, deeggarsi yeroo gabaabaa qofa osoo hin taane imaammanni kabaja namaa deebisu, mirga eegu fi mootummaa naannoo irratti amanamummaa haaromsu fayyadamummaa yeroo dheeraa qaba.
