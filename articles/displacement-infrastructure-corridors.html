Rights Groups Document Displacement Along Infrastructure Corridors

Mirga Namoomaa

Several non-governmental organisations have published detailed documentation of communities displaced by road, railway, and industrial corridor construction in Oromia. The reports call on government authorities to implement transparent compensation schemes and fair resettlement processes in line with international human rights standards.

Findings of the Reports

The organisations, working independently but drawing on overlapping research, describe patterns of rapid eviction with little or no advance notice, inadequate or unpaid compensation for farmland and homes, and resettlement to areas lacking basic services. In several documented cases, communities were relocated to land that proved unsuitable for farming — their primary livelihood.

"People are told to leave. Sometimes they are given a week, sometimes less," one field researcher said. "Their land is gone, and what they receive in return does not come close to replacing it."

Legal Framework and Government Obligations

Under Ethiopian law and international frameworks to which Ethiopia is a signatory, those displaced by development projects are entitled to fair and prompt compensation, meaningful participation in resettlement planning, and access to grievance mechanisms. Rights groups argue that these obligations are routinely not met in practice.

Authorities have stated that development projects are conducted within legal frameworks and that compensation processes are ongoing. Critics say the pace and transparency of those processes fall short of what affected families need.

Calls for Action

The reports urge independent monitoring of displacement and resettlement processes, the establishment of accessible grievance bodies, and increased involvement of affected communities in decision-making. International donors and development finance institutions financing related infrastructure are also called upon to apply human rights conditionality.

Barruu kun qophii yeroo ammaa ti fi gabaasa guutuu dhiheenyatti guddifama.

Dhaabbattooti mootummaa hin taane hedduun hawaasota daandii, baabura fi daandii industirii ijaarsa Oromiyaa keessatti buqqa'an ragaa qindaa'aa baasaniiru. Gabaasaaleen kun mootummaalee haayyama kafaltii iftoomina qabuu fi deebii qubsumaa mirgaa tartiiba mirga namoomaa addunyaatiin waliin ta'e hojiirra oolchuuf gaafatu.

Argannoolee Gabaasaalee

Dhaabbattooti kunneen, ifaan hojjetaa jiran iyyuu qorannoo walmakatan irratti hundaa'uun, addaaddummaa araddaa hatattamaa beeksisa xiqqaa ykn dhabamuu malee, kaffaltii qonnaa fi manneen gahaa hin taane ykn kaffaltii dhabuu fi deebii qubsumaa naannoo tajaajila bu'uraa hin qabneetti galmeessu.

"Namoota achi deemuu jedhamuu. Yeroo tokko torban tokko kennama, yeroo tokko dansaa dhabama," jedha qorataan dirree tokko. "Lafa isaanii dhabaniiru, fi wanti argatan bakka buusuuf hin geenye."

Dhimma Seeraa fi Dirqama Mootummaa

Seeraa Itoophiyaa fi mallattoolee addunyaa Itoophiyaan mallattessee jalatti, buqqa'iinsi sagantaa misoomaa dhiyaatee kaffaltii haqa ta'ee fi hatattamaa, hirmaannaa qubsumaa karoorfannaa keessatti fi mala komii argachuu mirga qaba. Gareewwan mirga namoomaa dirqamaaleen kunneen hojii irratti yeroo heddu hin guutamne jedhu.

Waamicha Tarkaanfii

Gabaasaaleen qo'annoo walaba ta'e qabiyyee buqqaatii fi qubsumaa irratti, dhaabbata komii hawaasota miidhamaniif argamuu fi murtiiwwan irratti hirmaannaa hawaasota miidhamaniif dabalamuuf waamicha godhu.
