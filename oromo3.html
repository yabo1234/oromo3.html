About Oromo3

Who we are and what we stand for.

Waa'ee Oromo3

Eenyuu akka taane fi maal irratti akka dhaabbanne.

🎯 Our Mission🎯 Kaayyoo Keenya

Oromo3 is dedicated to delivering accurate, timely, and unbiased news about the Oromo people, Oromia, and the wider world — in both English and Afaan Oromoo. Oromo3 oduu sirrii, yeroo isaa fi qulqullina qabu waa'ee saba Oromoo, Oromiyaa fi addunyaa bal'aa – Afaan Oromoo fi Ingliffaan – kennuuf carraaqna.

🌍 Our Vision🌍 Mul'ata Keenya

We envision a world where every Oromo speaker has access to news and information in their own language, bridging communities across the globe. Addunyaa keessatti kan hunda dura dubbataan Afaan Oromoo oduu fi odeeffannoo afaan ofii isaanitiin argachuu danda'u ilaalla — hawaasota addunyaa guutuu walitti hidha.

📰 Editorial Values📰 Gatii Editooriyaalaa

We uphold accuracy, fairness, independence, and respect for cultural identity in every story we publish. Oduu hunda keessatti sirrummaa, haqa, of-danda'ummaa fi kabaja eenyummaa aadaa eegna.

🤝 Community Focus🤝 Xiyyeeffannaa Hawaasaa

Oromo3 was built by and for the Oromo community — at home in Oromia and across the diaspora worldwide. Oromo3 hawaasa Oromootiin fi hawaasa Oromoof ijaarame — naannoo Oromiyaa keessattis ta'e diaspoora addunyaa keessatti.
