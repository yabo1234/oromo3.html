Maaliif Afaan Oromoo? / Why Afaan Oromoo?

Afaan Oromoo is the most widely spoken Cushitic language in the world and the third most spoken language in Africa, with over 40 million native speakers across Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, and diaspora communities worldwide.

Despite its reach, Oromo communities have historically lacked access to quality, up-to-date news in their mother tongue. Most digital news platforms default to Amharic, English, or other major languages — leaving Oromo speakers underserved.

Oduu AI Afaan Oromoo changes that. By harnessing artificial intelligence, we deliver timely, accurate, and community-relevant news directly in Afaan Oromoo — empowering speakers to stay informed, engaged, and connected to both local and global events.

"Afaan Oromoo ni dagaaga, oduu keenyas ni beekama."
